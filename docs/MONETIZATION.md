# Monetizing Sturum

Ways to generate revenue **without breaking trust** with students or the vision in [GOALS.md](./GOALS.md). Sturum’s current phase explicitly defers monetization until **core utility is proven**—this document is for when you are ready.

---

## When to start charging

| Signal | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| One department has **weekly active** students | Real habit, not launch-week signups |
| Materials and posts happen **without** you begging | Product value is real |
| Course reps or students ask to keep it running | Willingness to support exists |
| You can explain value in one sentence | Monetization story is clear |

**Do not monetize** an empty or dying community—fees will accelerate churn.

---

## North star for monetization

Revenue should **strengthen the department hub**, not extract from students who have no alternative.

| Aligns with Sturum | Conflicts with Sturum |
|--------------------|------------------------|
| Pay for **better discoverability** (search, AI, storage) | Paywall **basic** connection or messaging |
| **Institutions** pay for licensed access | Selling student data to advertisers |
| **Sponsors** relevant to students (books, tools, careers) | Intrusive ads in feed and DMs |
| **Premium** for power users (upload limits, analytics) | Fake engagement or pay-to-win visibility |

## Monetization models (ranked for Sturum)

### Tier 1 — Best fit (start here)

#### 1. Freemium for students (free core + paid Plus)

**Free (always):**

- Sign up, department feed, connect, message, browse and download materials within fair limits  
- Basic search  

**Sturum Plus (paid monthly / semester):**

- Unlimited uploads and larger file size  
- **Semantic / AI search** over materials ([AI_INTEGRATION.md](./AI_INTEGRATION.md))  
- “Ask Sturum” grounded Q&A with higher daily limits  
- Early access to features  
- Optional profile badge (low priority—avoid pay-to-win social status)  

**Pricing sketch (adjust for market):**

- Nigeria / similar: ₦500–₦2,000/month or ₦3,000–₦8,000/semester  
- Global reference: $2–5/month student plan  

**Why it works:** Students who already rely on materials pay for **time saved**, not for friends.

---

#### 2. B2B — Department / faculty / student association license

Sell Sturum as a **managed community hub** for one department or faculty.

**Buyer:** Department office, faculty association, course rep body, or student union chapter.

**Package includes:**

- Branded space (department name, optional logo)  
- Admin / moderator tools (reports, pin announcements, approve materials)  
- Analytics: active users, uploads, top materials (aggregate, privacy-safe)  
- Priority support and uptime  
- Optional SSO later (university email domain)  

**Pricing sketch:**

- Pilot: free or low-cost one semester to prove value  
- Then: **annual license** per department — e.g. $500–$5,000/year depending on size and features  
- Or per-active-student fee: $1–3/student/semester (minimum floor applies)  

**Why it works:** Matches [GOALS.md](./GOALS.md) wedge strategy—**win one department** with budget holders who already fund events, handouts, and reps.

---

#### 3. Institutional pilot (university innovation / ICT)

Partner with **one university** for a sanctioned pilot—not replacing LMS, complementing peer life.

**Offer:**

- Department-scoped deployment  
- Data processing agreement (DPA) and student privacy policy  
- Training for course reps  

**Revenue:** Pilot grant, innovation fund, or paid expansion after evaluation.

**Why it works:** Credibility and budget; slower sales cycle.

---

### Tier 2 — Good with guardrails

#### 4. Sponsored placements (not random ads)

**Examples:**

- Bookshop / publisher — “Recommended for [Department]”  
- Coding bootcamp, marine industry employer — **career** slot for relevant departments  
- Exam prep service — **clearly labeled** sponsored; max 1 unit per screen  

**Rules:**

- No ads in private messages  
- No tracking across the open web  
- Course reps can veto sponsors for their pilot  
- Label: **Sponsored** always  

**Revenue:** Flat fee per month per department slot, or CPM with low volume.

---

#### 5. Affiliate / referral (ethical)

Earn commission when students use links **you curate**:

- Textbooks, calculators, software (GitHub Student, Notion, etc.)  
- Career tools for marine / STEM cohorts  

**Rules:** Disclose affiliate relationship; only products you’d recommend anyway.

---

#### 6. Premium materials (marketplace — high care)

Allow **verified uploaders** (senior students, reps) to sell notes packs or curated past-question compilations.

**Sturum take:** 10–20% platform fee.

**Risks:** Copyright, scam uploads, equity (poor students priced out). Requires:

- Clear copyright policy  
- Refund rules  
- Human review for paid listings  
- Free tier still has community uploads  

**Recommendation:** Only after scale and legal clarity—**not** Phase 1.

---

### Tier 3 — Possible but weak or risky for Sturum

| Model | Issue |
|-------|--------|
| **Display ads everywhere** (AdSense) | Low revenue per student; hurts UX; wrong brand |
| **Selling personal data** | Destroys trust; likely illegal under privacy laws |
| **Paywall to message friends** | Students leave for WhatsApp |
| **Crypto / NFT / token** | No student problem solved |
| **Generic B2C subscription only** without B2B wedge | Hard to convert broke students at small scale |

## Revenue mix (example at maturity)

For a **single large faculty** pilot that grows:

| Source | Share of revenue (illustrative) |
|--------|----------------------------------|
| Department / association license | 40–60% |
| Sturum Plus (students) | 20–35% |
| Sponsored career/education slots | 10–20% |
| Affiliates | 5–10% |
| Paid materials marketplace | 0–15% (if launched) |

Early stage: **one B2B pilot + optional Plus** is enough.

## Phased monetization roadmap

### Phase 0 — Now (pilot)

- **Free for all** students in target department  
- Measure: weekly actives, materials uploaded, return rate  
- Collect feedback: “Would your department pay to keep this?”  

### Phase 1 — First revenue (6–12 months after habit)

- Introduce **Sturum Plus** (AI search + limits on free tier)  
- Close **one paid department license** with course reps or faculty  
- Optional: one **sponsored** career/education partner for that department  

### Phase 2 — Repeatable wedge

- Template sales deck for “department hub in a box”  
- 3–5 departments (same or different universities) on license  
- Refine Plus pricing from conversion data  

### Phase 3 — Scale

- Institution-wide discussions (multiple departments)  
- Moderation + admin product tier  
- Marketplace only if legal and ops are ready  

---

## Pricing principles

1. **Semester-aligned billing** — matches student cash flow.  
2. **Local purchasing power** — Nigeria vs UK vs US cannot share one price.  
3. **Free tier must stay useful** — or WhatsApp wins.  
4. **Transparent** — no hidden fees on uploads or messages.  
5. **Student discount** — verify with `.edu` or university email domain when possible.  

---

## Legal and trust checklist (before first payment)

- [ ] **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy** (what you collect, AI use, uploads)  
- [ ] **Copyright policy** for materials (user responsibility + takedown)  
- [ ] **Refund policy** for Plus and marketplace  
- [ ] **Payment provider** — Paystack / Flutterwave (Nigeria), Stripe (international)  
- [ ] **Business registration** if required in your country  
- [ ] **VAT / tax** advice for digital services  
- [ ] **Do not sell** email, phone, or DM content to third parties

## What to say publicly (positioning)

**Students hear:**

> “Sturum is free for your department community. Optional Plus helps you find materials faster with AI search. Your department can also support the platform so it stays ad-light and student-first.”

**Buyers (reps / faculty) hear:**

> “A department-scoped hub for coursemates, materials, and announcements—with moderation and analytics—not another global social network.”

---

## Metrics that matter for revenue

| Metric | Why |
|--------|-----|
| Weekly active users per department | Basis for B2B pricing |
| Materials uploaded / downloaded per week | Proves library value |
| Plus conversion rate (free → paid) | Validates freemium |
| Churn on Plus | Pricing too high or weak features |
| NPS or “would you recommend?” | Trust before ads |
| Cost per department to support | Margin on licenses |

---

## Summary

| Question | Answer |
|----------|--------|
| **When?** | After one department uses Sturum habitually |
| **Best first move?** | **Department license (B2B)** + **freemium Plus** tied to AI/materials |
| **Avoid early?** | Aggressive ads, paywalled chat, data sales, paid marketplace |
| **Long-term?** | Trusted department layer funded by students (optional) and institutions (main) |

**Monetize utility and trust—not attention.**
