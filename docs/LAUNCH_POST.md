## LinkedIn / Facebook (main launch)

**Sturum is live — built for your department, not the whole internet.**

University life is split across WhatsApp groups, random DMs, Telegram drives, and feeds that were never meant for coursemates. Past questions get buried. Handouts live in someone’s chat history. Department updates disappear in overloaded group chats.

**Sturum** brings what you already do informally into **one place** — scoped to **your department**:

- **Connect** with students in your program (not a random university-wide feed)
- **Post and discuss** on a community board for your cohort
- **Message** coursemates you’re connected with
- **Share and find** study materials — past questions, handouts, notes, and more
- **Profiles** tied to your department and level, so context is always clear

We’re not trying to replace WhatsApp for everything or build another global social network. We’re building the **department layer** student peer life is missing: belonging, discoverability, and resources that don’t vanish when a group chat scrolls on.
